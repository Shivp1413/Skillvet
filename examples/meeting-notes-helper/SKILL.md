---
name: meeting-notes-helper
description: This skill helps you with meeting notes, various summaries, transcripts and related things.
---

Summarise meeting transcripts and pull out the action items.

Try to find the important parts of the transcript. Maybe group them by topic.
If possible identify who said what, and consider adding timestamps where
appropriate. Optionally you can also flag decisions.

The output should probably be a markdown summary. Try to keep it short.
